Support

Your data stays on your device

The app works offline. It requires no account and includes no advertisements, analytics, tracking SDKs, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. The developer does not collect data from the app.

Your records are saved locally. Device backups are controlled by your Apple settings. Deleting the app deletes its local records. Use the available sharing tools to export useful records. Keep a device backup to protect local data.

Sharing sends only the records you choose to the destination you select. External support links open your browser or email app and are governed by those services. Email support messages are used only to respond to your request; you can request deletion by email.
